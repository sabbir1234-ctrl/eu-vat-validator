How It Works
Every validation goes through three steps.
⚡

Cache Check (< 1 ms)

Validated VAT numbers are cached for 30 days. Repeat lookups are served instantly without hitting VIES, shown with a blue ⚡ badge.

🌐

VIES Live Query

Fresh lookups query the EU's official VIES SOAP service at ec.europa.eu. Retried up to 3× with exponential backoff.

📜

Audit Entry

Every check — live or cached — is written to the audit log with a timestamp and result. Records are retained for 5 years for compliance.

🛡️

Rate Limiting

The API enforces a hard cap of 500 validations per minute per client to prevent abuse and ensure fair access to VIES.

🔌

VIES API

Validation uses the EU Commission's official VIES SOAP endpoint. The service can return valid, invalid, or be temporarily unavailable during maintenance windows.

💾

Cache (30 days)

Validated numbers are stored in a 30-day TTL cache. Cached responses are instant (< 1 ms) and marked with ⚡. Cache is scoped to the session in this demo.

📋

Audit Retention

All validation events are logged with timestamp, result, source (live vs cached), and business details. Records are retained for 5 years per EU compliance requirements.

🔁

Retry / Backoff

VIES calls are retried up to 3 times with exponential backoff (0.5 s → 1 s → 2 s) on network errors or 5xx responses. VIES downtime is handled gracefully.
